THE ARC

Eight weeks.

Hawaii got six. This one gets eight, and it needs them.

Four days of straight dancing in Waikiki are done. The reps carried over. The problem is that the room in Burlingame is better, so the same level that felt comfortable on the beach is going to feel average on a floor of 2,500.

Carlitos put a number on it. He rated the Hawaii floor at 5 to 7. He rates this one 6 to 8. One point does not sound like much until you are the weakest lead in a line of four. The ceiling is higher here, which means the good followers are better, the songs go longer, and nothing gets carried by the vibe.

WEEK 1Sep 21-27

Reset the base

Salsa
  • Basic, cross body, right turn, clean and slow
  • Find the 1 in unfamiliar tracks
Bachata
  • Weight change and hip, no arms
  • Basic to side step, no bounce
Private with Leela, Sep 25. Bring one question, not five. Ask her what breaks first when you get tired.
WEEK 2Sep 28 - Oct 4

Lead clarity

Salsa
  • Frame tension, same pressure every rep
  • Cross body with inside turn
Bachata
  • Lead the turn from the body, not the hand
  • Traditional footwork at speed
Drill: One social. Dance only with people better than you, and count how many times you had to fix a lead mid-move.
WEEK 3Oct 5-11

Add the second style back

Salsa
  • Shines, four counts, repeatable
  • Hammerlock chain
Bachata
  • Sensual: body wave into a turn
  • Two combos, banked
Drill: Record one song of each. Watch it back on mute. If the shape does not read without music, it does not read on the floor.
WEEK 4Oct 12-18

Musicality

Salsa
  • Hit the break, do not just keep stepping
  • Pause on purpose, then move
Bachata
  • Mambo section accents
  • Slow down to half time and hold it
Private with Leela. Bring the muted video from week 3. Let her pick what to cut.
WEEK 5Oct 19-25

Stamina

Salsa
  • Three songs back to back, no sitting
  • Keep the frame in song three
Bachata
  • Same, and watch the shoulders drop
Reality check: the floor at SBK runs to 7 AM. Four days of it. If you gas out in song three at a Tuesday social, you will not last a night there.
WEEK 6Oct 26 - Nov 1

Kizomba and zouk, at least the basics

New
  • Kizomba walk and saida
  • Zouk basic and head movement, follow side too
Keep
  • Salsa and bachata combos stay warm
Why bother: two of the four rooms are kizomba and zouk. Walking in with zero means paying for a pass you only half use.
WEEK 7Nov 2-8

Pressure test

Salsa
  • Freestyle to unknown tracks only
  • No rehearsed openers
Bachata
  • Dance with strangers, not the regulars
  • Ask for one correction per dance
Private with Leela. Last one where new material is allowed. After this it is polish only.
WEEK 8Nov 9-15

Polish, no new material

Salsa
  • The two combos that actually land, clean
  • Exits, so every move ends somewhere
Bachata
  • One sensual combo, one traditional, both boring-clean
Drill: run them until they are dull. Dull is the point. Dull survives a 2 AM floor when you cannot think.
PEAK WEEKNov 16-18

Sharpen, rest, pack

Do
  • One easy social, low pressure
  • Stretch, sleep, hydrate
  • Pack Tuesday so Wednesday is free
Don't
  • No new combos
  • No late nights
  • No panic practice
Land loose. The Thursday pre-party runs 10 PM to 3 AM and it is the first read the room gets on you. Arrive warm, not wrecked.
