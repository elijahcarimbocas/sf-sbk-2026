THE ENGINE

It already runs.

Hawaii worked because of the weeks before it. Same engine, eight more weeks, higher bar.

Four days of straight dancing in Waikiki went fine, and none of that came from the four days. It came from a schedule that was already running every week. The curveballs, the unfamiliar follows, the styles he had not seen before, all of it was survivable because the reps were already banked.

5 + 3
Every weeknight is a class. Friday through Sunday is a social. Five classes and three socials a week, counting the Saturday afternoon block. No beginner classes, ever. No Sundays at the studio, because Sunday is ladies styling and heels and he leads. That is the whole system, and it does not need reinventing for this trip. It needs holding.
